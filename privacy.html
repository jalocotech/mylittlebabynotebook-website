3. Information We Collect

3.1 Account Information

  • Full name, email address, encrypted password
  • Google account information (if you use Google Sign-In)
  • Biometric login preference flag; when biometric login is enabled, your login email and encrypted password are stored in the device secure enclave (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore) — this data never leaves your device and is deleted when you disable biometric login

3.2 Baby Profile Information

  • Baby's name, date and time of birth, gender, profile photo
  • Birth measurements (weight, height, head circumference, gestational age)
  • Your relationship to the child

3.3 Health Records — User-Entered Only

All health data in the app is entered solely by you, based on your explicit consent. The developer does not generate, infer, add, or access this data — it is stored solely in your own account. Data stored includes whatever you choose to record: vaccination dates and notes, supplement/vitamin/medication schedules, growth measurements, dental development dates, and visit notes/photos.

Important: The vaccination schedule and checkup reminders displayed in the app are general reference guides only — not medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional.

3.4 Daily Tracking Data

  • Feeding logs: breastfeeding (side, duration), pumping, bottle, solid foods
  • Diaper change logs with timestamps and notes
  • Diary entries (8 categories) with photos
  • Milestone and baby steps records
  • Tooth eruption records per FDI notation

3.5 Photos and Media

Photos edited using the in-app editor are processed entirely on your device, then uploaded to Cloudflare R2 storage. No editing metadata is sent to developer servers.

When you add a photo, its EXIF metadata (including capture date) is read on-device to auto-fill dates. This metadata is never uploaded — only the photo itself is stored, with EXIF stripped before upload.

3.6 Co-Parenting Feature

When you invite a co-parent, their email address is stored to manage shared access. It is not shared with third parties or used for marketing.

When you choose to share content (photos, PDF memory books, milestone images) via the system share sheet, the content is delivered to the third-party app you select. The developer has no control over how that app handles the shared data.

4. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to operate the app: authenticate your account, sync records across devices, schedule notifications, generate PDF memory books, display growth charts, and enable co-parenting sync. Your data is never sold, used for behavioral profiling, or shared beyond those listed in Section 5.

6. Data Retention

Data is retained until you delete it. Account deletion removes all associated data from our servers within 30 days. Individual entries can be deleted at any time from within the app.

7. Security

  • All data transmitted over HTTPS/TLS
  • Passwords never stored in plaintext — encrypted via Flutter Secure Storage
  • Photos protected by Firebase authentication tokens (Bearer)
  • Screenshot capture is blocked within the app (disabled in developer builds only)
  • Biometric processing occurs entirely on-device; biometric data never transmitted
  • Cloudflare R2 access gated by verified Firebase tokens

8. Children's Privacy

This app processes data about children under 13 only through their parents or legal guardians. We do not knowingly collect data directly from children. All accounts must be created and managed by adults (18+). If you believe data from a child was collected without parental consent, contact support@mylittlebabynotebook.app and it will be deleted promptly.

9. Your Rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time
  • Delete your account: Settings → Account → Delete Account
  • Opt out of personalized ads via device settings
  • Submit privacy requests to support@mylittlebabynotebook.app

Requests are responded to within 30 days.

10. Changes to This Policy

Updates will be posted on our website and within the app. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

Fotoğraf eklediğinizde, EXIF meta verisi (çekim tarihi dahil) tarih otomatik doldurma amacıyla yalnızca cihazınızda okunur. Bu meta veri hiçbir zaman yüklenmez — yalnızca fotoğrafın kendisi, EXIF soyulmuş biçimde saklanır.

Sistem paylaşım menüsü aracılığıyla içerik (fotoğraf, PDF hatıra kitabı, kilometre taşı görseli) paylaştığınızda, içerik seçtiğiniz üçüncü taraf uygulamaya iletilir. Geliştirici bu uygulamanın söz konusu veriyi nasıl işleyeceğini kontrol etmez.

6. Veri Güvenliği

  • Tüm ağ iletişimi HTTPS/TLS ile şifrelenir
