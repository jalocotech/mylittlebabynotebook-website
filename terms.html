5. Service Description

My Little Baby Notebook is a personal digital baby journal. Features vary by subscription tier:

Free Tier

  • 1 own baby profile + 1 co-parented baby profile (2 total max)
  • Memory Moments: photos and notes with 30+ category templates
  • Baby Milestones: photo-backed milestone records
  • Growth Tracking: weight, height, head circumference with WHO percentile charts
  • Feeding Tracker: breastfeeding (left/right, duration), pumping, bottle, solids
  • Diaper Log: wet/dirty/mixed with timestamps and notes
  • Health Schedule: age-based vaccine and checkup calendar with reminders
  • Supplement Tracker: add vitamins, medications or supplements — daily, specific weekday, or every-N-day reminders
  • Tooth Map: visual FDI dental notation jaw map
  • Baby Diary: 8-category rich text journal with photos and PDF export
  • PDF Baby Book: Classic style only, watermarked; 4 formats (A5 Landscape, A5 Portrait, A4, 25×25 cm)
  • Sleep Sounds: White Noise only
  • Co-Parenting: invite co-parents to your baby via email
  • In-App Photo Editor: crop (1:1, 4:3, 9:16) and rotate
  • Notification Center: all supplement, vaccine, checkup, and system alerts in one feed
  • CDC Developmental Guide: milestone tracker based on the CDC "Learn the Signs. Act Early." program — social, language, motor, and cognitive categories (ages 2–60 months)
  • In-app advertisements via Google AdMob

Plus Subscription (additional features)

  • Up to 5 baby profiles total — your own babies + co-parented babies combined
  • Invite co-parents to all your babies (up to 2 co-parents per baby)
  • Accept co-parent invitations — within your total 5-profile limit
  • Bulk photo upload up to 10 at once (free: single upload)
  • All 14 PDF styles: Album, Clean, Polaroid, Lined, Geometric Floral, Gingham, Scattered Hearts, Full Hearts, Hexagon Star, Polka Dots, Zigzag (+ Classic on free)
  • Date and category filters for PDF books
  • Watermark-free PDF and photo sharing
  • Full sleep sounds collection — 12 sounds: White Noise, Pink Noise, Brown Noise, Fan, Hair Dryer, Vacuum, Aircraft Cabin, Heartbeat, Womb Sound, Shush, Hum, Low Vibration; simultaneous dual-sound playback
  • Ad-free experience (AdMob SDK disabled)
